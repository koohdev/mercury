## 📋 Client Requirements: Moderator Tracking Portal## 👥 System Roles & Permissions (School Analogy)

* Admin (Teachers): Full authority. They oversee the platform, track logs, manage schedules, and monitor moderator activity.
* Moderators (Students): Limited access. Their interface is restricted strictly to the basic operational functionalities listed below.

------------------------------
## 🔐 1. Authentication & Onboarding

* Sign-In Method: Google Account (Gmail integration).
* Account Setup: Admin creates the user account and provisions a temporary password.
* Reference: image8

------------------------------
## 📊 2. The Dashboard (Moderator View)
A simplified control center stripped of all marketing, CRM, or campaign tracking features.
## ⏱️ Time-Clock Component

* Clock-In: Starts the shift timer.
* Clock-Out: Stops the tracker and prompts the shift summary.
* Break: Pauses the active timer for rest periods.
* (Note: Running clock and clock-out UI variations will be added once updated).

## 📈 Gamification & Core Metrics

* Monthly Pay: Running total of earnings for the current cycle.
* Shift Streaks: Counter for consecutive scheduled workdays completed.

## 📝 End-of-Shift Verification

* Mandatory Summary: A text popup appears instantly upon clicking "Clock Out".
* Constraint: The moderator must write a small paragraph summarizing their shift's tasks before the system registers the final clock-out.

❌ REMOVED SECTIONS:

* Needs Attention
* Always Active
* My Campaigns

## ⚠️ Conflicts & Discrepancies Identified

Based on reviewing the original client requirements document against the UI mockups (images), here are the conflicts and misaligned references:

1. **Sign-In Method vs. Login Screen (Image 8):**
   - **Document:** States "Sign-In Method: Google Account (Gmail integration)."
   - **Image 8:** Shows a standard Email/Password input form for sign-in, not a "Sign in with Google" button.

2. **Image References are Mismatched:**
   - **Auth & Onboarding:** Document references `image1`. Image 1 is the Sidebar. The login screen is actually `image8`.
   - **The Dashboard:** Document references `image3, image4`. Image 3 is Response Templates, Image 4 is Dashboard (top), and Image 7 is Dashboard (bottom).
   - **Navigation Tabs Management:** Document references `image5` (which is My Availability). The actual sidebar navigation is in `image1`.
   - **Team Schedule:** Document references `image6` (which is Log History). The correct schedule image is `image10`.
   - **Response Template:** Document references `image7` (which is Dashboard). The correct template image is `image3`.
   - **Log History:** Document references `image8` (which is Login). The correct history image is `image6`.
   - **Availability:** Document references `image9` (which is Settings bottom). The correct availability image is `image5`.
   - **My Pay:** Document references `image10` (which is Team Schedule). The correct pay image is `image11`.
   - **Settings:** Document references `image11` (which is My Pay). The correct settings images are `image2` and `image9`.

3. **Removed Sections Still Present in Mockups:**
   - **Navigation Tabs:** Document says "❌ REMOVED TABS: Active Campaigns, Campaign Notes, Task Log". However, `image1` clearly shows these tabs present under "TODAY" and "WORK".
   - **Dashboard Sections:** Document says "❌ REMOVED SECTIONS: Needs Attention, Always Active, My Campaigns". However, `image4` shows "NEEDS ATTENTION" and `image7` shows "MY CAMPAIGNS" and "Always Active". (This means these sections need to be hidden/removed in the actual development despite being in the mockups).

4. **Pay Frequency Discrepancy:**
   - **Image 2 (Settings):** States "Pay periods are biweekly (15th and end of month)."
   - **Image 11 (My Pay):** States "Mods are paid once a month, at the end of the month."
